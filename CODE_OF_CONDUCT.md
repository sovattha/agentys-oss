# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are acting in good faith. Critique code, not
the person who wrote it.

## What we expect

- **Judge contributions on their merits.** Disagree with an approach as much as
  you like; do not make it about the contributor.
- **Assume competence and good faith.** People come to this project with
  different backgrounds, languages, and levels of experience. A question that
  seems obvious to you was not obvious to them.
- **Accept that maintainers say no.** Not every contribution fits, and a
  declined pull request is not a judgement of you.
- **Keep it in English or French.** Both are used in this project. Do not
  penalise anyone for writing in their second language.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, or commits
- Comments demeaning someone for their identity, background, or experience level
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies to every space the project occupies — issues, pull requests, commit
messages, discussions — and to anyone acting as a representative of the project
in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it to the maintainers
privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form,
which is the private channel available on this repository.

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will review the report and
respond in a way they judge proportionate — from a private word, through
removing comments, to blocking a contributor from the project.

Maintainers who do not uphold this code in good faith can expect to be held to
it themselves.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is written for this project. It is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), which is worth
reading if you want a fuller and more formal treatment.
