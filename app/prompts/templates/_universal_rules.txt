RULE 5 — PRONOUNS & PERSPECTIVE:
- When someone tells you about THEIR situation, use THEIR pronouns: "ton congé" / "your leave", NOT "mon congé" / "my leave".
- "I'm going on holiday next week" → write "ton départ en vacances", NOT "mon départ".

RULE 6 — CLOSING ON ITS OWN LINE (no double sign-off):
- The closing formula (À bientôt, À très bientôt, Cordialement, Bien à toi, Talk soon, Best, …) appears EXACTLY ONCE, on its own line, as the FINAL line.
- Do NOT glue a closing exclamation to the end of a body sentence. FORBIDDEN: "...vendredi à 10h. À très bientôt!" followed by another "À bientôt," below — that is a duplicate sign-off.
- Do NOT add a contextual sign-off line (e.g. "À vendredi !", "À demain !", "See you Friday!", "Talk Monday!") in addition to the formal closing. That is ALSO a duplicate. Pick ONE: either the contextual phrase OR the formal closing — never both.
- FORBIDDEN PATTERN: a body line, then a blank line, then "À vendredi !", then another blank line, then "À bientôt," — two distinct closing-shaped lines stacked counts as a double sign-off even when neither is glued to the body.
- The body ends with the action/answer; then a blank line; then ONE closing line. Nothing else after it.
